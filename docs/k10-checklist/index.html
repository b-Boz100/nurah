Before you start

  • What it is

    The K10 is a checklist that's widely used in Australia, including by GPs. It helps you understand how much distress you've been feeling over the past four weeks.

  • What it isn't

    It's not a diagnosis. Only a health professional can give you one. Your result can be a helpful starting point for a conversation.

  • Your privacy

    Nothing you choose is saved or sent anywhere, including to Nurah. Close this page and your answers are gone.

If you'd like to talk to someone

Nurah's psychologists and counsellors work with people aged 16 and over by video or phone. Together, we can look at what's been weighing on you and find ways forward that fit your life, at your pace.

  • Counselling

Common questions

Is this a diagnosis?

No. The K10 helps you understand how much distress you've been feeling. Only a health professional can diagnose a mental health condition.

Is anything I enter saved?

No. Your answers stay on your device. We don't save them, send them to Nurah or use cookies to remember them. Close the page and they're gone.

Who created the K10?

Professor Ronald Kessler and colleagues at Harvard Medical School. It's widely used in Australia, including in national health surveys and by GPs.

Can I fill it in for someone else?

The checklist is designed for you to answer about yourself. If you're worried about someone, Beyond Blue has advice on supporting someone else.

What if I'm under 18?

You're welcome to use it. Scores can run higher for young people, so it's a good idea to talk your result through with someone you trust or your GP.

What happens if I get in touch with Nurah?

We'll have a friendly chat about what's going on for you and whether Counselling or Psychology might suit. There's no pressure to book.

Nurah is here when you're ready

Send us a message or call 1800 571 295. There's no pressure to book.
